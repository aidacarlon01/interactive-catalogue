**Interaction name:** `card-grid-selector`

**Purpose.** Give the learner a way to compare parallel dimensions of a concept and read detail on each one without leaving the page.

**Content** — complete each slot with your material. The interaction's built-in labels and prompts stay as they are.

Title: …
Body copy (intro): …

Card 1 heading: …
Card 1 one-liner: …
Detail 1 heading: …
Detail 1 content: …
Detail 1 worked example: …

Card 2 heading: …
Card 2 one-liner: …
Detail 2 heading: …
Detail 2 content: …
Detail 2 worked example: …

(repeat the block for each card)

You can place 2–10 cards using this interaction.

When the content is complete: copy and paste the whole brief into the SB and assign a CU ticket.

---

**Technical details for the LT** — don't update unless you want the interaction to behave differently.

**Layout.** The title with the body copy beneath. Below, the cards in a two-column grid (one column on narrow screens), each carrying its heading and one-liner. Below the grid, a detail panel.

**Interaction.** Clicking a card shows its detail panel below the grid: an eyebrow label "DETAIL", the detail heading, the detail content, and a worked example line. Keyboard: arrow keys move focus between cards (roving tabindex, wrapping), Enter or Space activates; Home and End jump to the first and last card. The grid is an ARIA tablist and the panels are tabpanels.

**States.** One per card. Exactly one selected at a time; card one is the default. All panels exist in the markup; only the selected one is shown. The selected card is filled and carries a tick badge as the non-colour cue.
