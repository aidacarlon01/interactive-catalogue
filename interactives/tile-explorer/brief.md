**Interaction name:** `tile-explorer`

**Purpose.** Encourage the learner to explore all facets of a topic, rewarding full coverage with a completion panel, rather than letting them read only one.

**Content** — complete each slot with your material. The interaction's built-in labels and prompts stay as they are.

Title: …
Body copy (intro): …
Feature strip headline: …

Tile 1 label: …
Tile 1 stat (number + unit): …
Tile 1 info block A (heading and sentence): …
Tile 1 info block B (heading and sentence): …
Tile 1 reflection prompt: …

Tile 2 label: …
Tile 2 stat (number + unit): …
Tile 2 info block A (heading and sentence): …
Tile 2 info block B (heading and sentence): …
Tile 2 reflection prompt: …

(repeat the block for each tile)

Completion panel heading: …
Completion reflection points (one per line, up to 3): …

You can place 2–6 tiles using this interaction.

When the content is complete: copy and paste the whole brief into the SB and assign a CU ticket.

---

**Technical details for the LT** — don't update unless you want the interaction to behave differently.

**Layout.** The title with the body copy beneath. Below, a filled feature strip carrying the headline, then a progress row (an "x of N explored" count, a progress bar, and a reset button). Below that, two columns on desktop: the tile grid on the left (each tile has an icon, its label, and an Explore/Explored status line), and an insight panel on the right. On mobile the insight panel stacks below the tiles. At the bottom, the completion panel, hidden at rest.

**Interaction.** Clicking a tile marks it explored (status changes to "Explored" with a tick icon — the non-colour cue) and shows that tile's insight in the panel: an eyebrow, the big stat counting up from zero (instant when reduced motion is preferred), the two labelled info blocks, and the reflection block. Exploring every tile reveals the completion panel with the reflection list. Reset clears all exploration and returns the insight panel to its starting prompt. Tiles are ordinary buttons in tab order; the progress count is announced via a live region.

**States.** The insight panel has one state per tile plus a starting prompt, exactly one visible at a time; the starting prompt is the default. Each tile is independently unexplored or explored (explored persists until reset). The completion panel is hidden until every tile is explored. All panels exist in the markup.
